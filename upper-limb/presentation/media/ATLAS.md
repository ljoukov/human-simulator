# Opening panel anatomy

The twelve `atlas-*.webp` stills are renderings of the walking atlas at 27% of optical stride `vicon-subj130-trial-1`. They support three anatomical layers and four camera angles when 3D rendering is unavailable.

`gpu-body-mesh.webp` shows the same recorded pose from the oblique camera, with the triangles of the outer body surface visible. It supports the whole-body modeling slide when 3D rendering is unavailable.

`science-walking-pose.webp` shows the atlas at 30% of Grand Challenge stride `ngait_og5-stance-1`, viewed from behind and to the right. This is the recorded pose used for the [whole-body force experiment](../../../whole-body-study/README.md#what-moves-first-when-hip-muscle-force-falls).
